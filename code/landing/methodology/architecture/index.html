Trust & Transparency

AI Twin separates what it knows from what it infers and what it predicts. Every memory can be reviewed, corrected, or removed.

KNOWN

"You usually exercise at 6 PM."

INFERRED

"You appear to prefer exercising in the evening."

PREDICTED

"You are likely to exercise at 6 PM today."
